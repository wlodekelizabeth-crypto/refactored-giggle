Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do I need any experience?

Not at all. Every workshop is made for beginners, and we guide you through each step.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. Aprons, tools and every material are ready on your table. Wear something comfy you don't mind getting a little waxy.

Can I come with friends?

Yes please! Book together on WhatsApp and we'll seat you side by side. For groups of 6 or more, ask about a private session.

Are children welcome?

Our public workshops are for ages 14 and up because we work with hot wax. We're happy to plan a kids' session for a private event.

Where are the workshops held?

At our studio in Dubai. You'll receive the exact location with your booking confirmation.

Can I order custom gifts or favours?

Of course. Send us your date, quantity and colours on WhatsApp. For large orders we suggest booking at least 2 weeks ahead.

Ready to make something lovely?

Message us on WhatsApp or come say hi on Instagram. We'd love to have you.
